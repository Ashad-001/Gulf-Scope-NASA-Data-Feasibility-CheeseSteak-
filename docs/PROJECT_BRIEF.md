# GulfScope

GulfScope is a public-facing environmental trend investigation project for the 2026 NASA International Space Apps Challenge, [Be An Earth System Trend Detective!](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/earth-system-trend-detective/).

The Bahrain case study asks whether near-surface air temperature and precipitation changed over time in the NASA POWER grid sampled at 26.07° N, 50.55° E. The initial period is January 1981 through December 2025. A future analysis should quantify trend magnitude and uncertainty and account for seasonality and serial dependence.

Mission 001 succeeds when the repository contains a reproducible retrieval and validation script, the authentic NASA response and provenance, and documentation of parameter availability, units, coverage, missingness and scientific limitations. Trend estimation, data integrations beyond NASA POWER, and application development require a later mission.
