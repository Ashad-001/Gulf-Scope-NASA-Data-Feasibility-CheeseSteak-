# Submission references

## NASA products and consumed records

- **NASA Langley Research Center, Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER).** Monthly AG point meteorology, 1981–2025. POWER meteorology is derived from MERRA-2 reanalysis. [Monthly API](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/monthly/) · [Meteorological sources](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/) · [Processing](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/processing/). Original meteorology requests and SHA-256 metadata are paired in the [raw archive](../data/raw/). Main response bodies: [Bahrain](../data/raw/nasa-power-bahrain-2026-11-14T12-43-27.267Z.json) · [Riyadh](../data/raw/nasa-power-riyadh-1981-2025.json) · [Muscat](../data/raw/nasa-power-muscat-1981-2025.json) · [Kuwait City](../data/raw/nasa-power-kuwait-city-1981-2025.json) · [Doha](../data/raw/nasa-power-doha-1981-2025.json) · [Abu Dhabi](../data/raw/nasa-power-abu-dhabi-1981-2025.json). The raw archive also contains mean daily minimum, additional meteorology and POWER solar records. [Solar sources](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_solar/)
- **NASA Global Precipitation Measurement, IMERG via NASA POWER.** Integrated Multi-satellitE Retrievals for GPM, daily UTC `IMERG_PRECTOT`, 2001–2025. [NASA GPM product information](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/imerg) · [POWER IMERG specification](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/#imerg) · [POWER daily API](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/). Consumed daily responses: [Bahrain](../data/raw/nasa-power-bahrain-imerg-daily.json) · [Riyadh](../data/raw/nasa-power-riyadh-imerg-daily.json) · [Muscat](../data/raw/nasa-power-muscat-imerg-daily.json) · [Kuwait City](../data/raw/nasa-power-kuwait-city-imerg-daily.json) · [Doha](../data/raw/nasa-power-doha-imerg-daily.json) · [Abu Dhabi](../data/raw/nasa-power-abu-dhabi-imerg-daily.json). Each body has a matching metadata file. The exact algorithm version and run were not supplied by the consumed response.
- **NASA POWER high-resolution SMAP Level-4 daily UTC Zarr archive.** Surface and root-zone volumetric soil moisture estimates. [POWER hydrology methodology](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_hydrology/#smap) · [Consumed archive metadata](https://nasa-power-smap.s3.amazonaws.com/smap/temporal/power_smap_daily_temporal_utc.zarr/.zmetadata) · [Archived metadata copy](../data/raw/power-smap-zarr/.zmetadata) · [Verified six-site extraction and provenance](../data/raw/power-smap-zarr/point-extraction.json). POWER archive metadata identifies v10.0.0, and it does not give a specific upstream SMAP version. [NSIDC product family](https://nsidc.org/data/spl4smgp)
- **Global Modeling and Assimilation Office, 2015.** MERRA-2 tavgM_2d_slv_Nx monthly time-averaged single-level diagnostics, v5.12.4, NASA GES DISC. Bahrain pilot uses native T2M from the February and March 2025 granules. [Dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.5067/AP1B0BA5PD2K) · [Earthdata collection](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules?p=C1276812859-GES_DISC) · [Verified cell extraction, exact filenames and SHA-256](../data/atlas/bahrain/merra2-t2m-pilot.json) · [Retrieval and reproduction notes](../data/atlas/bahrain/README.md). Original authenticated granules are kept out of Git. Giovanni was evaluated as a candidate pathway. The pilot uses GES DISC granules and no Giovanni export is documented.
