# Demo walkthrough

1. Open GulfScope and select **Begin investigation**. The opening illustration introduces the Bahrain NASA POWER case study.
2. Inspect mean air temperature. Hover or use the keyboard to inspect a year, or open **View annual values**.
3. Narrow the displayed period, then reset it. The report identifies the full period used for the trend.
4. Switch to mean daily minimum air temperature and inspect its estimate and interval.
5. Switch to precipitation and read the non-significant result and block-length sensitivity.
6. Pan or zoom the map and check the selected coordinate and model resolution.
7. Read the methodology, then expand the source record to show NASA retrievals and checksums.

The statistical conclusions apply to the gridded series. They do not establish local station changes or attribute them to a cause.
